## Main points
1. **Thesis.** The three roles of language can form one contribution, but the paper still reads partly as three studies attached to a dashboard; the common audit operation is asserted more than demonstrated. Expected reviewer sentence: *"a conventional linked CLIP dashboard wrapped around several unrelated negative results about prompt rewriting and VLMs."* Best answer: one reproducible end-to-end case where the same interface takes an axis claim, a rewrite claim and a VLM statement to the images, and an analyst decides better than with a competitive baseline.
2. **Novelty.** Auditing default shifts (DefaultShift), discovering language-described model differences (CompCon), set-difference captioning (VisDiff), diversity measurement (GRADE, DIMCIM), rewriting for diversity (Prompt Expansion, prompt modifiers) and linked exploration (ViBEx, Varif.ai) are established. The increment is the same-family guidance vs distillation comparison with per-subject location/spread, human-qualified axes, and language-claim tracing. "None offers a visual route back to images" is too categorical; add a compact feature comparison. Related work is thin on VA provenance and uncertainty, human validation of embedding axes, equivalence testing and VLM-judge calibration; references 19–22 are still unverified.
3. **Q4.** Supports, for the official upsampler, two generators, these prompts and validated aspects, *no resolved widening beyond the margin*; not equivalence or zero benefit. "Extra variety comes from sampling text" overreaches beyond the one fixed-rewrite design. Qwen rewrites were not validated in round 3.
4. **Q5.** The null control was added after the first answers were scored: exploratory. VLMs saw 8 vs 8 (16 vs 16) images, the audit used 32 seeds, so some "wrong" answers may describe real sample fluctuation; compare with an 8-seed audit or with people reading the same grids. Q5 truth is agreement with the audit, not independent visual truth. Supported: *these two VLMs, with this prompt and these grids, reported shifts in 98–100% of same-configuration controls.* Not supported: "a VLM always finds a difference"; "descriptions carry no information about whether models differ" (only false-positive rates were measured; Mistral reads variety direction at 0.79 and mentions narrowing with recall 0.88).
5. **Q6.** The proposed properties failed this CLIP operationalization; that does not show they are bad perceptual properties. "People understood them" needs a direct measure.
6. **Measurement and statistics.** The human check screens direction on selected large-gap pairs; it does not validate distances, IQR ratios or the 0.5 threshold. Show the four-annotator sensitivity and both round-2 passes in the main paper. "Same place" (unadjusted equivalence) and displacement (Holm) should be visibly different inferential categories. Reversal counts are case finders.
7. **Number and wording mismatches** (executor verified each): Q6 marked both final and pending (lines 5–7); Table 1 note "36 judgements per aspect and round" does not hold for round 3; "every case … answered with a shift" and "every pooled no change … described as a pooled shift" are false for Qwen (12/13, 49/50); "two-thirds of the candidate axes failed" (round 1: 8 of 15); "in every intervention narrowing was larger" conflicts with Q4's per-seed rewrites (spread ratios above 1, unresolved); the 38/60 count has no derivation file; `results/q5/RESULTS.md` still prints the unrestricted population (n = 369/251/80, 13/14, 86%) while the manuscript uses the restricted one (320/235/70, 12/13 and 13/13, 88%); Q3 medium's sub-margin shift (−0.47) should not vanish from the interpretation; the expert session is up to about 75 minutes, not 55; the expert-study training case uses background on rewritten images, an axis round 3 rejects.
8. **Visualization.** A plausible VA contribution in coordinated audit work, but familiar encodings and linking are not enough without evidence that coordination helps. Indispensable figures: an annotated whole screen with persistent scales, validity and settings; one guidance case; one pooled-reversal case; one language-claim case. Misleading: the map's x is relative to a cross-configuration median; package arrows look causal; edge triangles hide outliers; rank-matched lines are quantile alignments; the README still describes t-SNE density contours; 400 vs 10,000 bootstrap resamples; numbers only in tooltips.
9. **Expert study.** Not yet a comparative evaluation: five people from the authors' network, author-chosen cases, answers from the same audit. Before sessions: some outside experts, record prior involvement, a stronger baseline (sortable aspect-aligned images, per-subject summaries), blinded independent judges for a subset of answers, balanced cases, a piloted realistic schedule, a valid training case. Otherwise report a formative qualitative study without comparative accuracy claims.
10. **Integrity and reproducibility.** Deviations are disclosed, no sign of concealment; but outputs are traceable, not independently reproducible: add a manifest (model revisions, seeds, prompts, image and score-table hashes, library versions, batch size, data access route).
